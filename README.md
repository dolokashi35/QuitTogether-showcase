# QuitTogether

🏆 **2025 Congressional App Challenge Winner**

QuitTogether is a mobile app that helps teens and young adults quit vaping. It combines a personalized AI coach, a gamified progress tracker built on behavior-change techniques, and a local rehab-facility finder.

## Features

- **Personalized AI coach (QuitBot):** a Gemini-powered coach that sees your streak, recent cravings and slips, remembers the conversation, and replies in English, Spanish, German or Arabic.
- **Crisis-safety guardrail:** messages that signal self-harm or a medical emergency skip the AI entirely and show 911, the 988 Lifeline, the SAMHSA helpline and a vaping quit line.
- **Gamified progress tracking:** vape-free streak, money saved, puffs avoided, XP and levels, and 21 achievement badges.
- **Daily check-ins:** log craving strength and slips. A slip restarts the streak but keeps your best streak and badges, so setbacks don't erase progress.
- **Craving tool:** a 2-minute urge-surfing timer with guided box breathing.
- **Find support near you:** enter a zip code to see nearby treatment centers.

## Tech stack

Flutter (FlutterFlow) · Firebase Auth, Firestore & Storage · Google Gemini
